ZEROBASEONE (Korean: 제로베이스원), abbreviated as ZB1 (Korean: 제베원), is a South Korean boy group. It was a 9-member, temporary boy group with an activity period of 2 years and 8 months, selected through Mnet's reality show "BOYS PLANET." The group name was chosen through a recruitment result from the viewers of "BOYS PLANET," called STAR CREATOR, symbolizing "a free journey from 0 to 1, a brilliant and beautiful beginning." Their greeting is "D1, Be the ONE! Hello, we are ZEROBASEONE!" (Korean: D1, Be the ONE! 안녕하세요 제로베이스원 입니다.), which means "D1: We approach each day with the excitement and passion as if it were the first day; Be the ONE: Becoming the person we want to be together with our fans." The official fandom name is "ZEROSE" (Korean: 제로즈), which combines ZEROBASEONE and ROSE, representing that the members and fans are one. Together, they form ZEROSE.
